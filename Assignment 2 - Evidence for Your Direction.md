# Assignment 2: Evidence for Your Direction

**Week 2 | Submit one PDF | Pass/Fail**

In Assignment 1, you chose a provisional topic. This week, use **4–6 relevant sources** to decide whether to keep or revise your research question or project objective. The goal is to explain what the evidence changes, not to prove a research gap or finish your thesis design.

## What to submit

1. **Starting point.** State your provisional research question or project objective in one sentence. If you changed topics since Assignment 1, briefly say why.
2. **Search and selection record.** Note where you searched, the search terms you tried, and how you decided which sources to keep or set aside. If you changed your search terms, say what prompted the change.
3. **Source comparison.** Compare 4–6 relevant sources in one table, using the same headings for each source. Open the original sources and record the page or section that supports each entry. A search snippet or an AI summary is not a substitute for checking the source.
4. **What is known and what remains open.** Briefly compare what the sources agree or disagree on, what might explain differences, and what they leave unanswered. For a project, distinguish what has worked elsewhere from what has not yet been checked in your setting. A limited search does not show that nobody has studied the issue.
5. **Decision and next check.** State whether you are keeping or revising your question or objective, write out its current wording, and explain how the comparison led to that decision. Name one concrete next check that addresses an uncertainty you identified.

### Suggested comparison table

| Source (citation and DOI or link) | Setting, data, and method | Finding relevant to my direction (page or section) | What it does not tell me |
|---|---|---|---|
| One row per source | Who or what was studied, where, and how? | What does the original source actually support? | What is still unclear or may not apply to my setting? |

## How this is marked

**Coherence is the only grading criterion.** A reader should be able to follow why your search produced these sources, how your comparison supports your decision to keep or revise your direction, and why your next check follows from what remains uncertain. Keeping your direction is just as valid as changing it. You do not need a confirmed research gap, a final method, or a completed project design.

**Pass = 4 points; Fail = 0 points.** There is no partial credit. The five assignments are worth 20 points in total. **No late submissions are accepted.**
